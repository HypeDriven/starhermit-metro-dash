How to Play

Rules

  • Barrier (striped, low) — jump over it or change lane.
  • Sign (overhead panel) — slide under it or change lane.
  • Kiosk (full height) — change lane; jumping and sliding won't help.
  • Tokens — score bonus; high tokens need a jump.
  • Speed rises with distance. One crash ends the run.
